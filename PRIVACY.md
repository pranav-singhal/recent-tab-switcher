# Privacy Policy: Recent Tab Switcher

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Recent Tab Switcher does not collect, store, sell or send any personal data.

- **Tab titles, URLs and favicons** are read only to show the switcher list on your screen. They never leave your browser.
- **Your shortcut setting** is saved with Chrome's built-in sync storage, so it can follow you across your own Chrome profiles. No one else can see it.
- The extension makes **no network requests** and uses **no analytics or tracking**.

**Why it asks for access to all websites:** the switcher list is drawn on top of the page you're viewing, and it needs to notice when you press and release your shortcut keys. It doesn't read or change page content.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/pranav-singhal/recent-tab-switcher/issues
